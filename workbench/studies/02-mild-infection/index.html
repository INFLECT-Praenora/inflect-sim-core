Mild infection (severity 1)

Q Under a mild infection (severity 1), does the SBML extraction reproduce the BioGears engine's course — a controlled infection that resolves without progressing to hypotension — run for run?

Claim

Under mild infection both the real engine and the SBML extraction keep the infection controlled over 48 h — neither crosses into hypotension (no SBP<100, no MAP<65, no HR>90) — and the inflammation core matches almost exactly (tissue integrity min 0.9676/0.9676, peak pathogen 6.298/6.298). Mild is the agreement case that frames the severe-arm divergence.

Background

Question Under a mild infection (severity 1), does the SBML extraction reproduce the BioGears engine's course — a controlled infection that resolves without progressing to hypotension — run for run?
Hypothesis Both sides control the mild infection within ~24 h with SBP never falling below the healthy baseline and tissue integrity staying high; any divergence is small.

Model & approach

Run severity 1 on both the BioGears engine (viva-biogears) and the SBML extraction (viva-tellurium) over 48 h, align the 9 observables, and compare the milestone table (resolution time, SBP floor, tissue-integrity minimum).
