Claim

The Workbench evaluates pre-declared acceptance criteria against committed evidence and records a traceable verdict for each. Using the study-05 severe-arm milestones as the evidence source (evaluate_acceptance.py -> out/acceptance_eval.json), four criteria were declared with tolerances and evaluated: INFLAM-01 (minimum tissue integrity agrees to within 1%) passes at 0.0044% relative error; INFLAM-02 (peak blood pathogen within 1%) passes at 0.12%; HEMO-01 (time to MAP < 65 mmHg agrees within 6 h) fails at 15.3 h apart (engine 69.3 h vs SBML 54.1 h); and SURR-01 (surrogate hemodynamic accuracy within 10% of the exact engine) is pending — its validator has not been run. Two pass, one fail, one pending, each linked to its evidence artifact or its missing-evidence note.

Why: The supported verdict is scoped to the method: committed evidence flows through declared criteria to traceable pass/fail/pending verdicts, with at least one of each, each linked to an artifact or a named missing test. It does not claim the sepsis model passes overall. Two of its gates do not: HEMO-01 fails (the hemodynamic divergence from study 05) and SURR-01 is pending (the surrogate accuracy validator is unrun). Reporting those honestly, beside a completed demonstration verdict, is the behavior the study is meant to show. The follow-up that would close SURR-01 is a held-out, multi-seed surrogate-vs-exact trajectory comparison (study 04); the follow-up that would turn HEMO-01 into an explained result rather than a bare failure is the mechanistic perturbation test (study 05).
