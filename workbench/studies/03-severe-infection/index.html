Severe infection (severity 3)

Q Under a severe infection (severity 3), does the SBML extraction track the BioGears engine's collapse sequence run-for-run, and how large is the known timing divergence (the extraction report saw the severe course lag by ~8-10 h against the paper)?

Claim

Run-for-run, the SBML extraction reproduces BioGears' severe-sepsis inflammation almost exactly (tissue integrity 0.325/0.325, peak pathogen 60.5/60.6; values at the 48 h horizon, the severe course not yet at its true peak/min) but its hemodynamic collapse arrives ~11 h LATE: SBP crosses 100 mmHg at 37.5 h in the extraction vs 26.6 h in the real engine (a 10.9 h lag), with the same ~11 h lag on the HR>90 compensation. This is the engine-vs-extraction comparison the extraction report could not make; it confirms and slightly exceeds its ~8-10 h estimate against the paper.

Background

Question Under a severe infection (severity 3), does the SBML extraction track the BioGears engine's collapse sequence run-for-run, and how large is the known timing divergence (the extraction report saw the severe course lag by ~8-10 h against the paper)?
Hypothesis Both sides follow the same qualitative collapse — capillary leak, compensation, then hyperdynamic decompensation — but the SBML extraction lags the engine by several hours on the hypotension milestones, attributable to processes the extraction does not translate (urine, fever vasodilation, lactate, intracellular water).

Model & approach

Run severity 3 on both engines over 48 h, align observables, and quantify the divergence: SBP<100 and MAP<65 crossing times, SVR-vs-baseline minimum, and the magnitude of the timing lag — the run-for-run comparison the extraction report could not do.
